Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

At Ageverse, accessible from our website , we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is stored.

By using Ageverse, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Ageverse may collect or process the following information when you use our services:

Google Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google profile photo URL

When you sign in using Google, Firebase Authentication is used to authenticate your account.

Age Calculator Information

  • Date of birth entered into the calculator.
  • Age calculation results generated from that date.

Your date of birth is stored locally in your browser using Local Storage and associated with your signed-in account on that device.

Profile Photo

If you upload a custom profile photo, it is processed and stored locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to Firebase Storage by the current version of Ageverse.

Preferences and Notifications

We may store your theme preference, birthday notification preference, and notification history in your browser's Local Storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide Google sign-in and account-related features.
  • Display your profile information.
  • Calculate your age and birthday countdown.
  • Remember your date of birth and profile preferences on your device.
  • Provide birthday reminder notifications when enabled and supported by your device and browser.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of Ageverse.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Firebase and Google Services

Ageverse uses Google Firebase services, including:

  • Firebase Authentication: For Google sign-in and account authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: To store account profile information, such as your name and email address.

These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Ageverse may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, and support advertising services.

If Google AdSense or other third-party advertising services are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements based on users' visits to this website and other websites, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Google may use advertising cookies to personalize ads where permitted by applicable settings and consent requirements.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: Google Advertising Policies

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate consent options for advertising and related technologies.

Ageverse may use third-party services such as Google Firebase and Google advertising services.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies.

Ageverse is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

6. Data Storage and Security

Ageverse uses Firebase services and browser Local Storage to provide its features.

Some information, such as your account profile details, may be stored in Firebase Firestore. Other information, such as your date of birth, custom profile photo, theme preference, and notification settings, is stored locally in your browser.

Information stored locally may be lost if you clear your browser data, change devices, or use a different browser.

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of online storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. User Control and Data Removal

You can:

  • Sign out of your Google account at any time.
  • Change your profile information through the available profile settings.
  • Change or remove locally stored preferences by clearing the relevant browser data.
  • Manage notification permissions through your browser or device settings.
  • Manage Google advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center.

Signing out does not automatically delete your Firestore profile information or locally stored information.

For assistance with personal information or data removal, please contact us using the email address provided below.
